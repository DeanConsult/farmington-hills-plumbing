Our Story

Two Decades of Plumbing Excellence in Farmington Hills MI

Five Guys Plumbing Farmington Hills is an association of licensed plumbers with over 20 years of experience in the plumbing industry. Our goal is to always provide the highest level of customer service, and our plumbers have been proudly serving Farmington Hills for two decades.

Our plumbers are licensed, trained professionals who receive extensive training and recertification covering the latest advancements in the field. We guarantee each of them will handle your plumbing need with the utmost care and quality, as if it were their own home.

On February 15, 2021 every plumber in our company attended a nationwide conference on new innovations in the plumbing industry, and on June 4, 2021 we brought in trainers from our two main product manufacturers for a full day of in-office training. Staying on top of the newest technology is something we pass directly into our work.
